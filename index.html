Humanoid Robot Planning & Control

Core algorithm lead · Oak Creek · Jan 2026 — Apr 2026

02 / Learning-based Control

Wheel-Legged Robot RL Control

Algorithm & electronics lead · Oct 2025 — Present

A blind-locomotion control stack for a compact wheel-legged robot, combining leg-length-scheduled predictive control, proprioceptive state estimation, reinforcement learning, and embedded deployment.

  • Derived Newton–Euler full-body dynamics and built a leg-length-scheduled LPV-MPC model to optimize constrained control across changing robot configurations.
  • Used a TCN over proprioceptive history for blind state representation and velocity estimation, then trained terrain-robust locomotion policies with PPO.
  • Trained in Isaac Gym, validated in MuJoCo, deployed to STM32H7, and used PACE + CMA-ES to identify motor and joint dynamics from sim-to-real response error.

Outcome: velocity-estimation error below 0.05 m/s under strong disturbances and 0.6 ms policy inference on STM32H7.

03 / Semantic Localization

Persistent LiDAR Global Localization

Algorithm design & implementation · Jun 2025 — Mar 2026

A persistent semantic LiDAR localization framework for GNSS-denied urban driving that treats delayed one-shot hypotheses as global-prior proposals rather than direct pose resets.

  • Reframed one-shot absolute poses as asynchronous global-prior candidates; semantic-weighted ICP maintained continuous odometry while iSAM2 fused verified constraints.
  • Sampled particles in a bounded neighborhood and scored same-label scan-to-map matches with a weak vertical residual to jointly estimate pose correction, covariance, and confidence.
  • Propagated delayed priors and covariance along buffered odometry, admitting them through a 99% xy–yaw chi-square consistency gate.

Outcome: 96.64%–100% success at 5 m / 10° across ten MulRan and HeLiPR query sequences; on the HeLiPR Roundabout ablation, persistent fusion reduced median / maximum yaw error from 2.78° / 27.02° to 0.93° / 4.38°.

04 / Autonomous Navigation

MID360 LiDAR Navigation for RoboMaster

Navigation lead · RoboMaster 2025 · Sep 2024 — May 2025

A modular autonomous navigation stack for a competition sentry robot, integrating multi-sensor localization, path search, trajectory optimization, and constrained chassis control.

  • Integrated wheel-odometry prediction and LiDAR scan-matching observations in a tightly coupled ESKF.
  • Combined iVOX hash-based nearest-neighbor search with improved JPS, keeping 3D safe-corridor construction below 5 ms in complex scenes.
  • Generated high-order continuous collision-free trajectories using MINCO and L-BFGS, then tracked them with an LMPC enforcing power and wheel-speed constraints.

Outcome: 3D safe-corridor construction under 5 ms; RoboMaster regional first prize and national second prize.

05 / Robot Modeling & RL Control

UMV Bicycle Robot Model & Motion Control

Modeling & control · Sep 2026 — Present

An open-source, simulation-ready reconstruction of an underactuated bicycle robot, connecting image-based 3D modeling and URDF engineering with reinforcement-learning control for balance and dynamic skills.

  • Built a simplified six-link, five-joint model through multi-view generation and Blender assembly, with joint frames, inertial properties, visual meshes, and low-complexity collision geometry.
  • Formulated balance and velocity tracking as a constrained PPO task: proprioception and motion commands drive steering, head/neck position targets, and rear-wheel velocity through low-level PD control.
  • Combined task and motion-regularization rewards with hard safety terminations, curriculum learning, dynamics randomization, 3D stunt guidelines, and iterative motion imitation.

Outcome: Released the editable Blender scene, URDF, visual and collision meshes, and package-integrity checks for simulation development.
