01 / Humanoid Robotics

Humanoid Robot Planning & Control

Core algorithm lead · Oak Creek · Jan 2026 — Apr 2026

A real-time planning and control stack for humanoid navigation, combining candidate trajectory search, safe-corridor construction, and constrained nonlinear model predictive control.

  • Built a lattice local planner with collision-checked, cost-ranked trajectory candidates.
  • Designed an extended state observer (ESO) to estimate and compensate lumped control errors arising from model mismatch between the NMPC's lightweight system-identified motion model and the closed-loop behavior of the RL locomotion controller.
  • Formulated multi-obstacle avoidance as hyperplane constraints in an Acados NMPC controller.

03 / Semantic Localization

Persistent LiDAR Global Localization

Algorithm design & implementation · Jun 2025 — Mar 2026

A persistent semantic LiDAR localization framework for GNSS-denied urban driving that treats delayed one-shot hypotheses as global-prior proposals rather than direct pose resets.

  • Maintained a continuous pose stream with semantic ICP odometry while global localization ran asynchronously.
  • Designed a particle-based verifier using same-label semantic consistency to estimate proposal correction and uncertainty.
  • Propagated delayed priors with buffered odometry, then admitted statistically consistent priors into an iSAM2 factor graph through an xy–yaw gate.

Outcome: 96.64%–100% success at 5 m / 10° across ten MulRan and HeLiPR query sequences; on the HeLiPR Roundabout ablation, persistent fusion reduced median / maximum yaw error from 2.78° / 27.02° to 0.93° / 4.38°.

04 / Learning-based Control

Wheel-Legged Robot RL Control

Algorithm & electronics lead · Oct 2025 — Jan 2026

Developed full-body control and state-estimation methods for a compact wheel-legged robot, targeting robust locomotion under terrain changes and external disturbances.

  • Derived the Newton–Euler full-body dynamics and formulated a full-state LTV-MPC problem for balancing and terrain-adaptive locomotion.
  • Developed an Encoder–Res-TCN that jointly predicts body velocity and heteroscedastic uncertainty from proprioceptive history, and trained the locomotion policy with PPO.
  • Trained in Isaac Gym, validated sim-to-sim transfer in MuJoCo, and compressed the policy network through knowledge distillation and quantization for deployment on STM32H7.

Outcome: velocity-estimation error below 0.05 m/s under strong disturbances and 0.6 ms policy inference on STM32H7.
